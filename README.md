# Echoes of the Void

A mod that expands upon one of Minecraft's most mysterious and often overlooked features: the void. This mod transforms the void into a realm of its own. With dark secrets, new mobs, structures and dimensions. Why leave it just as a mere limit when it can be a gateway to expansion?

This mod achieves to offer a new experience to the game while feeling something that would be in vanilla minecraft.

## Mobs/Entities

Deep within the lowest levels of the world, near the bedrock, dwell the Void Shadows--rare entities which emerge only in extremely dark conditions. They appear briefly, but their presence is ominous, and they become hostile if disturbed. Normal weapons won’t harm them, as they require special methods to defeat.

To subdue these entities, you must craft a Void Chamber. This block traps Void Shadows and allows you to harvest Shadow Fabric, an extremely dangerous material essential to unlocking the deeper mysteries of the void.

To craft the Void Chamber, though, you need special materials. Since this mod is designed as optional exploring which adds some QoL improvements, all of these items shouldn't be hard to get at the late game--when you're supposed to begin contemplating the idea of exploring the Void.

The mod-related items you can get while in the overworld are:

- **Shadow Fabric:** Dangerous material that conforms Void life beings. It is a base material for advanced crafting recipes. It is extremely volatile when it contacts normal matter, and you need a special upgrade to your armor to be able to wield it normally.
- **Ominous Mold:** A rare item found in Ancient Cities, Trail Ruins and less often in Nether Fortresses.
- **Ominous Template:** A non-duplicable armor template for making an armor piece "Void Resilient". A player with a Void Resilient armor set equipped is able to carry Shadow Fabric normally. Dropping it is still dangerous, though. It does not affect the armor aesthetically.
- **Shadow Seal:** An item crafted with an Amethyst, an Ender Eye, and 7 Dark Stone. It is used to make the Void Chamber
- **Shadow Extractor:** An item crafted with a Sculk Catalyst, a Diamond, and an Ominous Mold.
- **Void Chamber:** An item crafted with a Shadow Extractor, a Shadow Seal, 4 Obsidian, and 4 Echo Shards. When a Void Shadow is nearby, it'll attract it to be absorbed within the block. After 30 seconds (upgradeable), it'll generate Shadow Fabric based on which kind of Shadow it was (more on that later). There are also two slots in the UI. One slot is for "tainting" a compatible item to contain Shadow Fabric. The other one is used for upgrading the effective area or speed of the Void Chamber.

## The Void Realm

The Void Realm is the main dimension of the mod which is a large complex of chaotic valleys and islands floating on the Void itself. Above it, there's the Shadow Spikes (where you fall from when you enter the dimension until you make something specific); a limit between the bedrock and the true Void. Below it, there's the Limbo; the other dimension of the mod. The Void Realm is the place where Void Shadows come from, and the cause of the Void Fog in the lowest levels of the world. In order to access it, you need to make or find a breach. Breaches are weak spots in the normal fabric of the world generated when a Void Shadow de-spawns. They can form on any block, but they're only stable on Bedrock (normally, it just applies a cool purple texture and then explodes). To make one yourself, drop shadow fabric in a bedrock block. This only works on the overworld (because the Nether Roof). If you have a Void Resilient armor, you can access the breach without fear. Now you know what truly is beneath the bedrock.

In this *new* dimension, you're able to find several biomes:

- **Forgotten Wastelands:** An empty wasteland with no mobs. Only some materials and ores can be found, but they're not common nor abundant. They represent things like old features, and can be a metaphor to the negative side of Minecraft's community (I'm a Persona fan with the soul of an artist; symbolism is my second language).
- **Lonely Islands:** These are small chunks of land Islands floating in the middle of emptiness. They sometimes have Hollow Trees and some rare, but useful ores are only found here. It is easier to fall to the Limbo from there.
- **Hollow Forest:** Based slightly on the Persona 4 Golden dungeon (no one will notice it, right?). These are a series of woods with some structures, and some of the most unpredictable, mysterious, and dangerous entities are found here. They are full of Hollow Trees and some statues--distorted versions of some mobs and/or structures found across all dimensions except the Limbo.
- **Shadow Dunes:** A biome with custom generation and structures. There are few entities here, but the ones that dwell here are hard to spot and a handful of them are dangerous. Not as much as Hollow Forest's, though. They represent the emptiness of Minecraft worlds when they are forgotten by players.
- **Ominous Peaks:** They are tall cliffs with abundant valuable ores in their sides. The fall from them, though, is always deadly or takes to the Limbo. They can generate inside other biomes unlike most of the aforementioned ones. There is only one entity here, but it is neutral. There's also a rare structure here.
- **Oblivion Plains:** More frequent the closer you are from (0, 0). The entirety of the dimension in a radius of 3500 blocks is just this biome. Neutral and Peaceful entities are the most common here as most, if not all, of the biome is composed of some sort of structure or mega-structures. There are no ores or useful materials here. The only dangerous entities are those from said structures.
- **Redeemed Fragment:** A small and extremely rare fragment of overworld land sitting below the Spikes, but higher than the rest of the biomes. It's the only way of getting overworld blocks once inside the dimension.
- **Corrupted Fragment:** Also a small and extremely rare fragment of land. But it is at the lowest points of the dimension, and it is conformed of a corrupt version of an overworld biome. It is... there. It actually serves no purpose.

The structures generated in this dimension are:

- **Within the Lonely Islands:**
  - Ruined Hut: Small abandoned hut with a chest. Rarely appears on the larger islands of the biome.
- **Within the Hollow Forest:**
  - Corrupt Statue: A small statue resembling a distorted version of a mob; usually a peaceful one.
  - Corrupt Temple: A larger distorted version of the Jungle Temple with one of the strongest entities.
- **Within Shadow Dunes:**
  - Empty Oasis: Small safe spot from the biome entities. Can have an abandoned camp with loot.
- **Within the Ominous Peaks:**
  - Cliff Site: Rare structure at the side of a cliff. They contain mining tools for the ores found there.
- **Oblivion Plains:**
  - Watch Tower: Small tower dungeon (larger than a Pillager Outpost) with loot and hostile mobs. They are the most frequent the further away you are from the Void's (0, 0) coordinate.
  - Town Echo: A small town resembling villages. You can find neutral entities here along some loot. They're a bit more complex than villages. They are more common on a 1500 block radius from (0, 0).
  - City Echo: A huge complex city that covers the entirety of the dimension in a 500 block radius. It ranges from small houses to big buildings (Minecrafty style, obviously). All houses may have loot, but bigger buildings can have hostile entities instead of the neutral ones in Town Echoes. At the center, there's the big clocktower. At the top, there's  the only natural exit to the dimension: the null point. Some important sub-structures are:
    - Time Echo: A sub-structure of the City Echo. They are small clocktowers scattered around the place with valuable items required to craft most items that are found nowhere else.
    - Home Echo: A small dirt/wood hut in the suburbs of this mega-structure. They are a nod to most players' fist base.
    - Grind Echo: A medium-sized tower with redstone contraptions and sometimes simple overworld farms (although they do not work). A nod to technical players.
    - Game Echo: A stadium-like structure with hostile entities from around the dimension. A nod to PvP players.
    - Law Echo: A set of structures that are like a city hall/bank. They represent late-game structures and how players often "give on-demand jobs" to villagers.
    - Safety Echo: Prisons containing some kinds of mobs due to what they represent. They also contain distorted versions of overworld mobs. They represent the prison community (no chunkbans, sorry).
    - Null Tower: The aforementioned huge clocktower with the Null Point at the top. The climb is filled with dangerous mobs and mini-bosses who guard the only exit. The Null Point is the only way out of the dimension until you unlock another item(s).
